## Beyond Finance: Five Immediate Edge-AI Plays

1. **Healthcare Triage** – On-device agents that read patient SMS lab results and route urgent cases before the doctor even opens her laptop.  
2. **Field Logistics** – Phones that translate work-order texts into real-time inventory updates in remote areas with no signal.  
3. **Retail Loyalty** – In-store devices that summarise purchase histories from receipt emails/SMS and personalise offers instantly—without sending PII to the cloud.  
4. **Education** – Student tablets that convert WhatsApp homework pictures into structured progress reports for teachers.  
5. **Compliance** – Edge agents that scan incoming vendor messages for AML red flags before funds are released.

All of this is feasible **today** with quantised local models, *retrieval-augmented generation* (RAG) for deep on-device knowledge, tool-calling, and a sprinkle of agent orchestration.

## Why Pocket-AI Beats Cloud AI (for Many Use-Cases)

* **Latency** – Sub-10 ms response feels like intuition, not software.  
* **Privacy** – Data never leaves the device; perfect for regulated industries.  
* **Cost** – Zero per-token fees once the model is downloaded. Scale to millions of devices without scaling servers.  
* **Resilience** – Works in airplane mode, disaster zones, or spotty rural coverage.  
* **Knowledge Depth** – With on-device RAG, your model can reference private PDFs, manuals, or policies without ever uploading them.
