## Meet FinanceIQ & SpendWise: The AI Agent Team Living in My Pocket

I built two specialized AI agents that collaborate entirely on-device:

| Agent | Role | Processing Time |
|-------|------|----------------|
| **FinanceIQ** 💎 | Parse raw SMS → extract `{amount, direction, counterparty, timestamp, tx_id}` | **≈6 ms** |
| **SpendWise** 🧠 | Classify transaction → allocate to budget envelope | **≈4 ms** |

Here's the magic: When an M-PESA SMS arrives, **FinanceIQ** immediately extracts the transaction details, then hands off to **SpendWise** who categorizes it and updates the appropriate budget envelope. **End-to-end processing: under 20ms.**

I can literally watch them collaborate in real-time through the "Processing Theater" screen—FinanceIQ says *"📱 New M-PESA SMS received! Let me extract the transaction details..."* then SpendWise responds *"🧠 Transaction categorized as 'Transport' - updating envelope balance..."*

### The Envelope Revolution: Digital Cash Management That Actually Works

The SpendWise agent doesn't just categorize transactions—it allocates them to **digital envelopes** that mirror how our grandparents managed cash. When SpendWise sees a matatu fare, it automatically deducts from your "Transport" envelope. A restaurant purchase hits "Food & Dining." Spotify renewal comes out of "Entertainment."

**The result?** Color-coded dashboard showing exactly how much remains in each envelope, updated in real-time as SMS messages arrive. No manual entry. No forgotten transactions. No budget confusion.

But this is just the beginning. Imagine waking up to:

Not overwhelming. Not judgmental. Just **intelligent, actionable insight** from data you're already receiving—processed by AI agents that never send your financial data anywhere.
