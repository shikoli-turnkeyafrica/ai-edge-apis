## Three Lessons That Stunned Me

### 1. Transparency Beats Perfection
Users forgave the occasional miscategorization because they could *see* the AI's reasoning. When FinanceIQ explained why it thought a payment was "Transport" instead of "Food," people felt included, not confused.

### 2. Automation Amplifies Agency
Contrary to my fears, users didn't become passive. They became more engaged. When the boring work disappeared, they had energy for the strategic thinking.

### 3. Privacy Builds Intimacy
Every single user mentioned feeling safer because "nothing leaves my phone." This wasn't just about data security—it was about emotional safety. Money conversations are vulnerable; users needed to know they were talking to *their* advisor, not someone else's.
