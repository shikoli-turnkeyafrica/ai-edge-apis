## Why Agent Teams Beat Super-AIs

### 1. **Specialization Creates Excellence**
A fitness agent that only thinks about movement will always outperform a general assistant trying to juggle fitness, finance, and family planning.

### 2. **Privacy Through Compartmentalization**
Your health agent doesn't need to know your bank balance. Your finance agent doesn't need your location data. Specialized teams create natural privacy boundaries.

### 3. **Failure Isolation**
When one agent makes a mistake, the others keep working. When a monolith fails, everything stops.

### 4. **Personalization at Scale**
You can swap out your **CareerWise** agent for one specialized in entrepreneurship while keeping your **HealthIQ** agent focused on marathon training. Mix and match perfection.
