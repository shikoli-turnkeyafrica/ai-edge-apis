**Meet FinanceIQ and SpendWise: My Pocket Financial Dream Team**

**FinanceIQ** is the extraction specialist. The moment an M-PESA SMS hits my phone, FinanceIQ springs into action. It reads the raw message and instantly extracts the transaction details—amount, direction, counterparty, timestamp, transaction ID. Every piece of structured data buried in that confusing SMS format.

Then **SpendWise** takes over. It's the categorization and allocation expert. SpendWise looks at FinanceIQ's extracted data and makes smart decisions: *Is this transport? Food? Entertainment? Bills?* Then it automatically allocates the transaction to the appropriate budget category.

The entire handoff happens in milliseconds, completely offline, with no data ever leaving my device.

But here's what made me realize I had built something special: **I can literally watch them collaborate.**

Through what I call the "Processing Theater," I see their conversation in real-time. FinanceIQ says *"New M-PESA SMS received! Let me extract the transaction details..."* Then SpendWise responds *"Transaction categorized as 'Transport'—updating balance..."*

It's like having two financial analysts who never sleep, never make mistakes, and never send your data anywhere.

**The Results Were Immediate and Shocking**

- I was spending **23% more than I thought** on subscriptions I'd forgotten about

- I had a complete cash-flow timeline across four institutions and three currencies that I'd never seen before
- Patterns in my spending that explained why I always felt broke on certain days of the month

But the real breakthrough wasn't just seeing where my money went. It was seeing where it was going *in real-time*, with context, and with intelligence.
